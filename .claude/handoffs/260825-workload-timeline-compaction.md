## Key decisions made

- **Compaction over row stability.** User chose to remove blank rows knowing lane count now
  follows the visible columns, so rows shift while scrolling.
- **Pack window quantises to the zoom's own column** (day at Week, week at Month, month at
  Quarter), NOT to a fixed week. Week-alignment was tried and collapsed _nothing_ on the real
  row — a viewport starting on a Saturday snaps back to Monday and re-admits the off-screen
  work it was meant to exclude. Recorded in `docs/FORK.md` because it looks obviously right.
- **Placeholders pack alongside dated work**, each occupying its single anchor day. The old
  one-per-row rule was half right: same-anchor placeholders do collide (the packer already
  handles that), different-anchor ones never did.
- **Both footer numbers are overflows over disjoint groups.** `Unscheduled` = estimated-undated
  (matches `meta.issues_unscheduled`); `Unestimated` = no-estimate regardless of dates. An item
  that is both belongs to the second.
- **Selection must run before packing** — `!target_date` is also the placeholder selector's
  predicate, so an uncapped row handed to `packTasksIntoLanes(tasks, todayISO)` would lane the
  entire undated backlog.

## Redis effectiveness finding (code reading, NOT measured against the bill)

Redis is doing almost nothing to shield Neon:

- Only **5** `@cache_response` decorators in the whole API: workspace labels, workspace
  estimates, and three license endpoints. Nothing on issues/projects/cycles/modules.
- **Zero** caching in all six fork-owned apps (`workload`, `views_ext`, `ai_ext`,
  `project_ext`, `cascade_ext`, `github_ext`).
- **Sessions live in Postgres**, not Redis: `SESSION_ENGINE = "plane.db.models.session"`
  extends `DBSessionStore`. Every authenticated request does a Postgres session lookup.

Redis's real jobs here are the Celery broker/result backend and the live layer.

Heaviest uncached reader is ours: `compute_workload` loads both querysets in full (measured
5,714 estimated + 3,724 unestimated rows on the busiest workspace) on every call, and the
timeline calls it once per scroll-settle gap while panning. Strongest candidate for both the
quota burn and the first cache to add, keyed on `(slug, granularity, window, filters)`.

**Not yet verified against Neon's per-endpoint transfer console — do that before changing
anything.** Also disclosed to the user: ~250 KB of `get_workload` responses were pulled against
production this session across five MCP calls (negligible against a GB-scale quota, but non-zero).
